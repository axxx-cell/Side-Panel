# Side Panel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Side Panel is a floating panel for Android with app shortcuts, a File Dock, notes and a calculator.

## What stays on your phone
- Your favorite apps, pinned files and folders, and notes are saved only on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.
- The File Dock stores only links to the files or folders you choose. Side Panel does not copy or upload them.
- Side Panel reads the list of installed apps only to show your app choices in the panel.

## What the app sends
- To check Pro status, the app downloads a public text file from GitHub. Your phone sends no personal data in this request, but GitHub may see your IP address as with any download.
- Your device code is made from your phone's Android ID mixed with the app name and shortened. It is shown in the app so you can send it to the developer if you donate for Pro.

## Permissions
- **Display over other apps:** shows the edge arrow and the panel.
- **Accessibility service:** used only to trigger the system screenshot and to remember which app was in front (the app name only) so the back arrow can return you there. Side Panel does not read, store or send your screen content or what you type.
- **Internet and network state:** used only to check the Pro list.
- **Notifications and run at start-up:** keep the panel running and bring it back after a restart.

## What we do not do
Side Panel has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data.
